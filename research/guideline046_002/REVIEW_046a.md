## Additional findings (this review)

- **N7 reference identity.** 046a's `cell:[^\s]+` cannot name a sheet whose name contains whitespace, and it does not define the binding. 046b uses inventory-assigned `cell:S<sheet index>!<A1>` IDs with exact, case-sensitive matching.
- **N8 canonical numeric text.** Rule 2 does not define how exponents or binary floats are handled. 046b defines it exactly: `repr(float)` → `Decimal` → fixed notation, with trailing fractional zeros and a trailing point removed; integers are written in plain digits.
- **N9 date, boolean, error and control-character cells.** A date-typed cell's cache is a serial number, and a text cell may contain control characters the writer rejects. 046b marks these `usable_for_raw=false` or makes them unrepresentable, and requires a hold instead of a repair.
- **N10 Q adoption.** Policy says that unverified adoption holds Q. 046b adds a mechanical guard: when the source adoption is not `verified`, Q must be null.
- **N11 classification domain.** 046a does not say whether blank or covered cells may be classified, or whether every content cell must be. 046b requires exactly one primary role for each content cell and forbids roles on empty cells.
- **N12 status mapping.** The top-level `partial` status has no carrier slot. 046b keeps it in carrier `metadata.response_status`, and items use only `research_draft` or `held`.
- **N13 self-check tool.** The product definition includes validation tools. 046b ships the stdlib-only checker/adapter as a package resource so that a generator can check its structure before returning. It reveals no source expectations and performs no repair.

## Consequence

046a cannot produce a carrier that the existing writer accepts for any item with A/B, components, numeric-typed text fields, or `partial` items, and it cannot carry proofs losslessly. It is retained unchanged as `FROZEN_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_NOT_DEPLOYED`. Fixes go into the new candidate `046b` in `research/guideline046_002/package/`, and 046b itself requires a separate fresh review before any trial.
