## Semantic review (FIELD_ROLES R47b–R59 govern; earlier rules where they do not disagree)

8. **Quoted range (R49).** The detailed bill sheet with the sender's prices is identified; the range starts at the first item line under the column header and ends at the row whose unit-price or amount column holds 合計 or 總計; a 合計 in the description column and a total labelled for a block (小計, 甲站合計) were treated as block totals; summary sheets yield no rows.
9. **Rows (R50).** Every priced and not-estimated line in range (not estimated: its unit-price or amount cell contains 不含估價, cited; a 以下不含估價 heading is not inherited; zero-state lines are not rows), and every unnumbered sender fee line before the end row that is priced or not estimated, gives exactly one row in source order. No line is split, merged or added. Lines with no price and no status, and item-like lines out of range, are `excluded_supply` with the stated reasons. Remarks saying "not included" did not remove a line. Supply and separate-contract markers did not decide inclusion and appear in no field. Any O10 omission (the panel line and its numbered descendants or R33 heading scope) is `excluded_supply` with the provisional O10 reason.
10. **Components (R51).** Components are rows only when their parent has no price and no status and its block lies inside the quoted range, or when every bill line has no price and no status; the parent then takes `section` and block totals are not rows. A no-price, no-status line whose block lies outside the range is `excluded_supply` and its block `analysis_unlinked`. Components of a priced or not-estimated parent stay `analysis_component` items or `analysis_unlinked` cells.
11. **C/D (R52, R53).** Segments split only at ，,、；;. The generic prefix named in the glossary and the category chain (only while a later noun segment remains) are dropped on every line type. C follows the order glossary, acronym, single segment, head noun, other: a glossary device gives its abbreviation and D = the rest or null; a digit-free Latin acronym of an equipment noun gives C alone (O20), while material, light-source, current and protocol tokens stay in D; a single segment otherwise gives C null; an equipment line otherwise gives the head noun; every other line gives C null. The equipment families are exhaustive and every glossary device is equipment. Only the prefix, the chain, C's text, markers, references and cost codes left the description; rating tokens are joined with single spaces on every line type (R53, tokens as defined there; SUS304 and IP65 are not rating-like); other punctuation is verbatim. Remarks, codes and price text are not in C or D. No abbreviation outside the glossary. R52 was applied to analysis components too. Acronym step: D = the rest after the noun and its acronym, or null (自動切換開關(ATS) 3P160A → C ATS, D 3P160A). A noun segment whose first token contains a digit (AC220V 指示燈) is handled as a description starting with a digit: C null, D = the text.
12. **I/J (R55).** Not-estimated lines carry I = J = 不含估價 from the cell that contains it. Any priced line carries a brand or model stated in its own cells (including the source's brand/model columns). With a 材料清單, I and J come only from its cells: no brand-table value appears, and the packet has no `context:brand_table` (O17). Only on a contract job, priced equipment lines without a stated brand or model take the brand-table entry by device type and rating band; a breaker whose rating no entry covers takes J = the table's breaker brand with I null (O18); a line stating only a brand or only a model takes the missing value only when the entry's brand matches; anything else is null with a reason. No brand or model from memory.
13. **Enclosure row (R56, O30).** A priced line supplying an enclosure (a 盤, 箱 or 櫃 housing devices, including instrument, isolation, sub-distribution, test and terminal boxes, the list not exhaustive; not 接線盒, 接線箱, 拉線箱, 既設盤, enclosure parts or services) is one row with A (the panel name of the heading or block title that names a panel: without the item or block label, a leading category segment and trailing 設備, 工程, 製作, 更新 or an enclosure count, removed repeatedly, it holds exactly one box noun and ends with it or with it plus a tag or one bracket, an enclosure word followed by 體, 內, 面, 門, 座, 板, 架 or 殼 ending no box noun; it goes to the first enclosure row in the scope that is not a meter, terminal, test, isolation or instrument box, else the first, the innermost heading that names a panel governing and component rows taking only their block title, unless the name has no tag and the row's own box noun has one; every other enclosure row, and every row without such a heading, takes the line's own noun phrase: every leading category segment and the glossary enclosure word dropped, a hyphen-joined enclosure word before a box noun dropped, qualifiers and tag kept, a tag directly after the noun kept, a 型/式 or tag parenthesis kept and any other parenthesis left to D or to the dimensions, a later bare panel tag before the noun, the last of two boxes, a 附…/含… box not a second box), B (`<w>W*<h>H*<d>D` in mm, no unit meaning mm, in W, H, D order whatever the source order, no unit or thickness, only from W/H/D or 寬/高/深 labels in the line's own description, the first of two complete sets; null with a reason for remark-only, partial, other-unit or absent dimensions), C (the glossary's enclosure word), D (the description without the prefix, every leading category segment, the text A took from the line and the dimensions; the line's noun stays when A came from a heading), E, F, Q, I null and J = the brand cell if stated, else the company name exactly; carried by a one-row physical_enclosure group. No other row carries A or B and no other group exists. A not-estimated enclosure is an ordinary row.
14. **Values (R48, R54, R57).** L, M and N equal the job inputs on every item (rows and analysis components) and each cites only its own context ref; M and N were not inferred from document furniture (R6, critical). K, O, P, G and H are null. Q is the unit price on priced lines and null otherwise. E and F are the line's own cells verbatim.
15. **Citations (R58).** Fields derived through a resource cite the source cell and the context ref; null reasons are true of the whole source (R9); total discrepancies are recorded, not repaired (R10).
15a. **Scoped packets (R59).** The bill sheet and end row are those of `context:scope`, not re-decided; every content cell on a bill-sheet row after the end row is `excluded_supply` ("outside the quoted range (R49, R59)") and no item cites one; scoped-out sheets are not treated as missing (no hold, no `held`/`partial` status or limitation for them) and nothing cites or reconstructs them; an analysis sheet is in scope only when every in-range bill line has no price and no status, and then its components are rows (R51); a no-price, no-status line whose block is on a scoped-out sheet is `excluded_supply` (A26, A37); and blocks of priced or not-estimated parents give no `analysis_component` items but `analysis_unlinked` cells with the R59 reason; `context:scope` is cited in no proof, raw selection, anchor, group or link (route `R59_SCOPE_FACT_CITED`). The evaluator classifies unmatched historical rows whose source lies on a scoped-out sheet, or below the end row, as scope loss.
