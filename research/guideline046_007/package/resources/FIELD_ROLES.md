## Types, groups and stage blanks (046b interface)

F, P and Q are JSON numbers or null; G and H are always null; every other populated value is a JSON string (write a numeric-looking specification such as `110` as the string `"110"`). A or B requires a group; an ungrouped item leaves A and B null with a reason.

**P1 Panel self-relationship** (declared 2026-11-03 before any 046b generation): A/B describe the panel that contains or contractually groups the item. A demand line whose supplied object is itself an enclosure or panel does **not** populate A/B with its own name or specification; its identity goes to C and its explicit dimensions/material to D. It receives A/B only through a separate explicit relationship to another identified panel. In flat numbering, a sibling enclosure line is not the panel of the lines after it.

**P2 Protocol/interface designations** attached directly to a device noun (for example a bus or port standard before or after the noun): no versioned policy yet decides between C (as part of the lexical name) and D. Place the token in exactly one of C or D, record its protocol role in the proof, and never discard it. Evaluation of this class must declare both placements prospectively.

## Naming and representation

Explicit name/spec columns are strong structural evidence, not a prohibition against recovering a name from a mislabelled/combined specification column when the complete item proves the role. Record the actual source cells and why allocation differs from their headings. Never invent a noun to fill C.

Apply the full naming policy and source-language default. Preserve generic source prefixes under this candidate's explicit policy. Preserve both general and specific product nouns unless a separately versioned policy relocates the general category into ancillary context without losing the specific name. No automatic historical-name aliases are approved.

For this cell-based candidate, raw multi-cell descriptions/remarks join proven continuation cells with literal newlines. Derived C/D/J replace actual newlines/continuation boundaries as R37 states; retain meaningful internal spaces and stored Unicode spelling. When D is assembled from source spans that are not contiguous in the source, join them in source order with U+FF1B only (rule R4 below). Trim leading and trailing whitespace at every C, D and J edge (R38). A separable boundary delimiter left at a new C/D edge is dropped (R4), and the transformation says so; meaningful signs, ranges and identifier characters remain. Separable wrapping parentheses that enclose a whole moved span (R14) stay in raw evidence, while the underlying qualifier remains in D. Parentheses integral to the technical expression are retained. The allocation rules below require independent semantic review before admission; see the 046d review record.

Do not remove a leading sign to satisfy a workbook writer. If a meaningful value cannot be represented safely by the admitted writer, hold the output and retain its exact value; do not silently omit or escape it into a different value.

## Allocation rules R1–R12 (046c, declared 2026-11-03 before any 046c generation)

These rules are generic and cite no contract. When a rule and an older sentence above disagree, these rules govern; when two of these rules disagree, apply R12. Revised after the independent semantic review of 2026-11-03; R1b and the R6 severity follow owner decisions of the same date.

**R1 Classify by predicate.** *Financial annotation*: a price, fee, tax, discount, payment, estimate status, who bears a cost (負擔), or the inclusion/exclusion of a price, fee or estimate (含運費, 不含估價). It never enters C or D; keep it in raw text and in a review reason. *Supply-basis qualifier*: the manner or source of providing the object (租用, 臨時, 新品, 既有, 業主供料, 另案). *Inclusion/exclusion of an object, accessory or work* (含底座, 不含安裝) is a D attribute. Always give a review reason for estimate status, cost bearing and other-party supply. A service or fee demand noun (安裝費) is C.

**R1b Supply-basis qualifier placement (owner decision 2026-11-03: D).** Remove a supply-basis qualifier from C together with its separable wrapping delimiters and place it in D as a separate span, always joined to other D spans with U+FF1B (發電機 租用 100KW → D 租用；100KW). Record "supply-basis qualifier" in the D proof. Never discard it.

**R2 Fused material/construction words.** A material, construction or type word written directly before the product noun with no delimiter, so that the two read as one product designation, stays in C. Move a material, finish, grade or construction word to D only when the source separates it from the noun (delimiter, separate cell, label) or when it carries a measurable value. In R2, R3, R7 and R8, whitespace alone is not a delimiter. A measurable value is a number with a physical unit (V, A, kVA, mm², 度), a pole count (P, 極), a phase count or phase/wire system (單相, 三相, 1φ, 3φ4W, 單相三線), or a grade code containing digits (SUS304). Letter-only abbreviations (PVC, XLPE) and stage, gang or circuit counts (段式, 聯, 路) stay in C. When fusion alone leaves the role unclear, keep the word in C and say so in the proof.

**R3 Counts.** A composition count stays attached to the sub-object noun it counts, in that noun's field: C when the sub-object is part of the name (按鈕開關三只組合盒); D when it is an included accessory introduced after a delimiter by 含, 附, 內含 or 包括 (含2只底座). A count or status word that limits how much work a service line covers — how many objects are installed, moved, removed or tested, or new versus existing — is a *scope count*; its placement is UNDECIDED pending review: keep it with its status word in exactly one of C or D, record "scope count; placement undecided" in the proof, and keep the work noun and its object in C. On a work line, new/existing words are R3 scope words; on an object line they are R1 supply-basis qualifiers (R1b). A count restating the item quantity (每盤1只) is cited in F's role_basis and its placement is undecided (R8 class "quantity-basis count"). Neither kind of count changes F or P or creates extra rows.

**R4 Joiners.** D spans are contiguous only within one source stream (description, specification or remarks) when every character between them stays in D; a newline or continuation boundary between them becomes one U+0020. Spans are non-contiguous otherwise, including when a removed wrapping delimiter or text placed elsewhere lies between them. Join non-contiguous D spans with U+FF1B only, ordered by stream (description, specification, remarks), then row-major, then by character. Remove separable wrapping delimiters around each moved span; keep delimiters that are part of the technical expression. For C, drop separators left at new edges (never half of a kept bracket pair), keep any delimiter between two retained spans, and add nothing (產品，端子台).

**R5 Document references.** A clause whose content is only a reference to a document, standard, drawing, catalogue or specification that is not in the source packet — including "per …", "refer to …", "according to …", "built to …" (詳…, 依…, 參照…, 按…規範) — is a document reference. Keep it in raw text and in a review reason or limitation. It never enters C or D and never supplies a value. If the same clause also states an explicit technical value, place only that value in D. A number that only names the governing document (CNS…, 台電規範, 圖號) is a reference even without 依/詳; a designation stating the attribute itself (IP65) is a value. A missing detail schedule that defines the demand holds the affected items (guideline §1).

**R6 Party roles.** A company, agency or person name that appears only in a letterhead, title, addressee line, page header or footer, stamp or signature block does not establish K, M or N. Only a label naming that relation to this order (訂購廠商; 訂購客戶 or 訂購單位; 使用客戶 or 使用單位) or contract text stating it establishes K, M or N; contract-party, contractor, supplier or quote-sender labels (甲方, 乙方, 業主, 供應商, 承包商) do not. Otherwise leave the field null with a reason that names where the name appears and that no role is stated. One party fills several roles only when each is separately labelled or stated. Inferring K, M or N from document furniture is a **critical error** (owner decision 2026-11-03).

**R7 Acronym beside a full name.** When the source gives a full name and, at a separate delimited position, an acronym, abbreviation or type designation, keep the full name in C and leave the acronym where its position puts it (D when it sits in a delimited attribute list). Put an acronym in C only when the source writes it as part of the name with no delimiter, or when it is the only name. A bracketed acronym directly after the full name (自動切換開關(ATS)) stays in C with its brackets (R47, owner decision O8). Never expand or translate either form.

**R8 Undecided classes.** (046f: R52 places protocol/interface tokens in D for the C/D procedure; C/D lies outside the governing match score (O19), and C/D evaluators may still declare both placements under O9.) When a token belongs to a class whose C/D placement the policy declares undecided (the classes listed at the end of this rule), the proof of the field that holds it must name the token's class (for example "protocol/interface designation; placement undecided"), and the null reason or proof of the other field must say the token was placed elsewhere. A token is in the protocol/interface class when it names a communication protocol, bus, port, network standard or transmission medium attached to a device noun, whether written before or after it, fused to it, abbreviated or transliterated. A medium word is in the class only when it qualifies a signal device; when it names the cable kind supplied (光纖電纜, 網路線), R2 governs. As in P2, the class covers attached tokens only; a delimited token is an ordinary D attribute. An undecided-class token kept in C keeps its source characters, including wrapping delimiters (電錶箱安裝（新設1只）); one moved to D follows R4 (新設1只); the other field never repeats it. The undecided classes are: protocol/interface designation, scope count (R3) and quantity-basis count (R3). The acronym gloss is decided (R47).

**R9 Truthful null reasons.** A null reason must be true of the item's whole logical scope (all its cells, its group heading and applied project-level text), naming any candidate text of that field's kind — including unresolved-role tokens — and where it was kept. Never state that the source has no such content.

**R10 Total discrepancies.** When a cached subtotal or total disagrees with the amounts it appears to sum, or its label leaves unclear whether it is a local subtotal or an overall total, record the observation and the ambiguity in limitations with the cells involved; judge discrepancies on canonical decimal values. Do not repair, do not recompute into any output field, and do not read a zero cache as a confirmed zero total.

**R11 Nonitem roles.** financial_summary: subtotal, total, tax, discount, management or insurance allocation and amount-in-words cells, plus the labels and qualifiers attached to those figures (such as tax-inclusion words beside a total). commercial_note: payment, delivery, validity, warranty, scope-option, supply, estimate or price-inclusion statements (以上單價含運費) that are not an item's own cell. document_reference: pointers to documents not in the packet. unresolved: two or more roles remain possible (name them) or no listed role fits (say why).

**R12 Precedence.** When rules send the same text to different destinations, the first applicable rule in this order governs: R11, R6, R5, R1 (financial annotation), R8, R3, R1b, R7, R2. Cite every matching rule in the proof. Never create a new undecided class during generation; record residual doubt in limitations.

**R13 Evaluator consistency gate (evaluator-side).** Evaluators may expect only conventions that follow from a sentence of this package, or that are covered by complete alternatives or exclusions declared before generation. Readers need not apply R13; it explains why R24 doubts are not scored.

## Clarifications R14–R24 (046c, declared 2026-11-03 before any 046c generation)

These answer generic questions raised during independent source-expectation authoring under the R13 consistency gate. They cite no contract and follow from R1–R12 and the published examples (N03, N08, N20, A13, A23). Where a clarification and R1–R12 disagree, R1–R12 govern. In the R12 order, R19, R20 and R23 rank with R5, and R16 ranks after R2.

**R14 Delimiters inside a moved span.** A moved span is the maximal run of consecutive characters in one stream that all go to D. R4 removes wrapping delimiters only when they enclose that whole run; delimiters inside it stay verbatim (A13, A25). R1b qualifiers are always separate spans.

**R15 Composing C.** Within C a bracket pair is kept or removed as a whole: removed when none of its content stays in C; kept otherwise. Two retained name spans keep the source delimiter between them; if none remains, they are joined with nothing (電纜｛45度｝彎頭 → C 電纜彎頭, D 45度).

**R16 Unidentified code tokens.** An unlabelled token of letters with digits that no other rule classifies — not a measurable value or grade code (R2), size or dimension designation (W100, DN250), attribute designation (IP65), protocol/interface designation (R8) or document number (R5) — is not part of C or D (e.g. QX-620 before a clear noun). Keep it in raw text and a review reason with its role unresolved (N03, N08, N20). A labelled code follows its label (型號 → I). A lexical name or acronym without digits (PLC-CPU模組, NFB) is a name, not a code token.

**R17 Fused measurable values.** A measurable value (R2) fused to a noun moves to D even without a delimiter (45度彎頭 → C 彎頭, D 45度; 100KW發電機 → C 發電機, D 100KW).

**R18 Condition words that name the object.** A condition word that says what the object is (廢料, 廢水, 污泥, 舊品 when it names the material handled by a service) is part of the object noun in C. R3 status words are new/existing/reused words saying which objects the work covers (新設, 既設, 利用舊品). A word naming the kind of work or the material handled (臨時用電, 廢料清運) is part of C.

**R19 Procedural clauses.** An instruction, coordination or approval clause (依監造指示, 配合現場, 經核可後) is treated like a document reference (R5): raw text and a review reason only; never C or D.

**R20 Conformity verbs.** 符合…, 合於…, conforming to … are document-reference verbs under R5. If the object of the verb is an attribute designation (符合IP65), place only that value in D (R5).

**R21 Analysis basis cells.** For a supported component link, `basis_raw_ref` is the component row's own quantity cell and `basis_unit_ref` is the component row's own unit cell. A block-header unit label is context for the link proof, not the basis unit.

**R22 Single-cell raw roles on multi-row items.** The unit, quantity, unit-price and amount raw cells are the item's single cells in those columns: the anchor-row cell. If that cell is empty, use the one continuation-row cell of the same item that has content there. When both rows have content in a column, the anchor-row cell is raw; the other is classified by its own role, or as unresolved naming the possible roles, and stays in the census.

**R23 Cost-line codes.** A pricing, resource or work-item code (工料編碼, 資源代碼, 工項編號) identifies a cost line, not a technical attribute: keep it in raw text; never C or D.

**R24 Remaining doubt.** Any C/D question these rules still leave open is recorded in limitations with the cells involved (R12). Evaluators exclude it from C/D correctness obligations unless complete alternatives were declared before generation; the next policy version decides it.


## Decisions R25–R44 (046d, declared before any 046d generation)

R25 corrects the analysis-component example found misleading in a 046c trial. R26–R38 decide the questions that independent source-expectation authors raised under 046c (policy backlog Q1–Q13); R39–R44 address generic error classes and disagreements observed in 046c development batch 1. They are generic and cite no contract; the examples are invented. They were revised after the independent semantic review of 046d. R25–R44 left the R8 classes unchanged; R47 later decided the acronym gloss. **Precedence:** R25–R44 govern R1–R24 and the older sentences above where they disagree. In the R12 order, R27 and R29 rank with R11; R35 with R5; R28 and R36 with R1 (financial annotation); R32 with R8; R31 with R7; R30 and R34 with R2; R39 and R40 with R1 (financial annotation); R41 with R1 (financial annotation), so it precedes R8, R3 and R1b; R42 with R5. R25, R26, R33, R37, R38, R43 and R44 are outside the order: they say how to compose or scope a result, not where a token goes.

**R25 Analysis component rows.** An `analysis_component` item carries its own row's literal unit in E and its own literal per-unit quantity in F: the same cells as its link's `basis_unit_ref` and `basis_raw_ref` (R21). F is the literal cell value even when the block's base exceeds one unit (每100M); never multiply or divide it, and never copy the parent's unit or quantity. Project-level values that apply to every item of the order (K, L, M, N, O, each only with the support the field table and R6 require) apply to component rows exactly as to demand rows. P stays null unless the component row itself states a required quantity; G and H stay null; Q stays null, because an analysis price is not an accepted contract rate. A component never carries A or B: they stay null with the reason "analysis component; A/B belong to 未填 rows (R25)". An analysis-block heading that names a panel is analysis context, not an R33 group. Example: a 單價分析 row 組立工資 | 工 | 2 under a heading that links it to a 式 item gives C 組立工資, E 工, F 2, and the same L as the demand rows.

**R26 One separator after a middle removal.** A separator is the whole run of delimiter and whitespace characters between two spans. When text removed from the middle of C leaves two or more separators between two retained spans, C keeps exactly one: the run that directly followed the left retained span (產品甲，100V，端子台 with 100V to D → C 產品甲，端子台).

**R27 Priced lines without an item number.** A line with its own unit, quantity or amount is a demand item, even without an item number, when it names an activity on the project's objects (設計, 製圖, 組立, 配線, 安裝, 測試, 試運轉, 搬運, 拆除) or a fee for one (運費, 安裝費), even when it adds a management word (試運轉及管理費 | 式 | 1 → demand, C 試運轉及管理費). A line that names only overhead or levies (管理, 品管, 安全衛生, 保險, 利潤, 稅, 雜費), or that states its amount as a percentage of other amounts, is financial_summary even with 式 | 1; a review reason then names any activity word in it.

**R28 Purpose and application phrases.** A phrase that only says what the object is for or where it is used (供加藥機使用, 機房用, 於二樓) is not a technical attribute: keep it in raw text, never in D, and name it in the D null reason when D is null (R9). Written as part of the product name with no delimiter (加藥機用泵浦), it stays in C (R2). A delimited 用-phrase that names an environment or protection condition (屋外用, 防爆場所用) is a type attribute under R30, like 戶外型.

**R29 Formula caches outside an item's own cells.** A formula cache on an item's continuation row, in its unit-price or amount column, is the item's raw cell and `demand_content` (or `analysis_content`) only when R22 selects it, that is, when the anchor-row cell in that column is empty. Otherwise, zero or not, it is `unresolved` with the reason "formula cache on a continuation row; the anchor-row cell is the item's raw <column> (R22)". A formula cache on a row that carries no item and no label is `unresolved` with the reason "formula cache on a row without an item; no listed role fits". A cache in a subtotal or total row is financial_summary.

**R30 Delimited kind or type words.** A delimited word that classifies the preceding product and cannot name an object by itself is a type attribute (D): words ending in 式, 型 or 形 (沉水式, 蝶型, 防爆型), spare words (備用, 預備), and delimited stage, gang or circuit counts (選擇開關，三段式). A delimited noun that can name an object stays in C with the preceding noun unless R3 makes it an included accessory, whether it is a more specific name for the same product (照明燈具，LED燈具) or a co-equal part (產品，端子台). Written with no delimiter, R2 governs (三段式選擇開關 stays in C).

**R31 Bracketed Latin tokens directly after a name.** This rule covers only a bracketed token of Latin letters, optionally with digits, hyphens, dots or slashes, written directly after a name; bracketed CJK text follows R1b, R2, R3, R28 or R30. Such a token is, in this order: a protocol/interface designation (R8; a bracket directly after the noun counts as attached for class membership only), a document number (R5), a measurable value, size or attribute designation (D, R2/R17/R34), an acronym gloss when it has no digit (R7, R47: it stays in C with its brackets), otherwise an unidentified code token (R16: raw text and a review reason).

**R32 Undecided-class tokens moved to D.** Wrapping brackets that enclose exactly an undecided-class token are always removed when it moves to D, so it forms its own span joined by U+FF1B (電力監視器(RS485) 220V with the protocol token placed in D → D RS485；220V). Otherwise it joins the maximal moved run (R14; A41). R1b qualifiers remain separate spans.

**R33 Panel names in section headings.** A section heading establishes a contractual panel group when its whole noun phrase, after the section number, names one panel designation: a panel noun, optionally with one tag, that panel's own specification, a count of identical enclosures, and one work word on that panel (工程, 製作, 更新) (三、控制盤; 一、動力盤P-1 2面). The group name is that noun phrase with its tag, without the section number, count or work word; B is the heading's own specification, otherwise null. A heading that names two or more panels, a panel with other objects or work (控制盤及配線工程), or a work category, location or system (機房設備工程) establishes no group: items under it keep A and B null, and the A null reason names the heading. Scope runs from the heading to the first of: the next heading of the same or higher level, a subtotal or total row, or the end of the table; when qualifying headings nest, the innermost governs. Every demand item in scope takes the group, including work on that panel (組立, 配線); the exception is a line supplying that panel or its enclosure itself, which leaves A and B null and cites P1.

**R34 Size designations and counts are measurable values.** A size designation is a dimension letter or symbol with a number (W100, DN250, φ50, #14), a number with a length unit or inch mark (2", 2B, 100mm), or a dimension product (100×60); a bare number is not one. Core or pair counts (芯, C, 對) are measurable values too. Fused or delimited, all of these go to D (R2, R17). An unlabelled bare number that no rule classifies is an R16 token (raw text and a review reason).

**R35 Standard numbers fused to a service name.** When a document or standard number is written directly before a service or test name, with or without whitespace (CNS1234耐壓試驗), the number is a document reference (R5: raw text and a review reason) and the service name alone is C (耐壓試驗). A body name without a number (IEC耐壓試驗, 台電檢驗) stays in C.

**R36 Mixed inclusion clauses.** In an inclusion or exclusion clause, words naming specific objects or work (底座, 安裝) are D attributes (R1). Cost words are financial annotations: words ending in 費 or 資, 費用, 稅, 人工, and a bare 材料, 料 or 工料 used as a cost category (含材料及工資, 連工帶料). Remove each cost word with the conjunction or separator that links it (及, 與, 和, 、); the inclusion word and the remaining objects form one D span joined with nothing (含工資及底座 → D 含底座; 含底座及工資 → D 含底座). The cost words go to raw text and a review reason. A clause left with no object is a financial annotation as a whole.

**R37 Continuation boundaries.** A newline or continuation boundary inside a span, together with any whitespace beside it, becomes one U+0020, or nothing when both neighbouring characters are CJK ideographs or full-width punctuation (附端子⏎蓋 → 附端子蓋; AC110V⏎附端子蓋 → AC110V 附端子蓋, where ⏎ marks the boundary). Do not judge word breaks otherwise.

**R38 Edges and verbatim copying.** Remove leading and trailing whitespace (U+0020, U+3000, TAB) at every C, D and J edge and record it in the transformation. Copy every other character that no rule moves, removes or replaces exactly, including internal spaces and an unpaired bracket at an edge.

**R39 Remarks about the item itself.** A remark that only excludes, cancels, suspends or marks the item as unused without naming an object or work (不含, 不使用, 免, 取消, 暫停) is not a technical attribute: keep it in raw text with a review reason. It never deletes the row, never changes F and never enters D; the item keeps status `research_draft` unless another rule holds it, and the D null reason names the remark (R9). A generic verb that refers to the item itself (施作, 設置, 使用, 採購) is not a named work (本項暫不施作 → R39). A remark that excludes a named object or work (不含底座, 不含安裝) follows R1 and R36.

**R40 Change remarks.** A remark that changes the item's method, material or type (改用不鏽鋼, 改為埋設) states a technical attribute: the change phrase as written goes to D. A justification or condition in the same remark (因現場…, 配合…, 依…) stays in raw text with a review reason (R19). A change does not delete the attribute it supersedes; each stays where its own rule puts it (電纜管，鍍鋅 + remark 改用不鏽鋼 → D 鍍鋅；改用不鏽鋼). A change that replaces the supplied object itself (改為接線盒) keeps C from the description, puts the change phrase in D and adds a review reason. A change of location or purpose follows R28.

**R41 Words inside inclusion clauses.** A status or count word inside an inclusion or exclusion clause (含新設線路2處) belongs to that clause. The clause as a whole follows R1 and R36; the word is not a separate R3 scope-count token, and R8 class naming does not apply. The proof cites R1 and names the word. This is a rule about class membership (such words are not R3 scope-count tokens), not a decision on the placement of any R8 class.

**R42 Holding for unavailable documents.** Hold an item (status `held`, values kept, the hold explained) when either: (a) after removing the document pointer and generic work verbs (施作, 施工, 辦理, 製作), no object or specific work noun remains (詳設計圖 | 式 | 1; 依規範施作 | 式 | 1); or (b) the pointer is to a missing detail schedule, list or quantity table (明細表, 附表, 清單, 數量表) (R5 last sentence; guideline §1). Otherwise, even with unit 式, do not hold an item whose own text names an object or specific work (配線工程 依圖施作 | 式 | 1; 電纜 3.5mm² 依圖施作): record the pointer under R5, and for a 式 item add a review reason that its extent is defined by the missing document.

**R43 Brackets at the start or middle of a moved run.** R4 removes wrapping delimiters only when they enclose a whole moved span. A bracket pair at the start or in the middle of a longer contiguous moved run is inside it and stays verbatim (R14): 電磁接觸器 (防塵型) AC220V → D (防塵型) AC220V. The only exceptions are R1b qualifiers, which are always removed with their wrapping delimiters as a separate span (A01), and R32: brackets that enclose exactly an undecided-class token.

**R44 Naming source columns in reasons.** Proofs and null reasons name a source column by its header text (quoted or translated) or its address, never by a document role the source does not state (R9).

## Owner decisions R45–R47 (046e, declared before any 046e generation)

These apply owner decisions O6–O8 of 2026-11-03 (`research/guideline046_005/DECISIONS_OWNER_O5_O9.md`). R45 and R46 apply after guideline §3, R11, R27 and R29 have identified demand lines, nonitem rows and analysis blocks, and before every allocation rule. They never make a nonitem row an item. R47 ranks with R7.

**R45 Which demand lines are 未填 rows (O7).** R45 sorts only the demand lines of the detail table.
- **Quoted price.** The line's unit-price or amount cell (R22) holds a number above zero (literal or cached) or text stating a positive amount (1,200元), or one of the line's own cells says its price is included in another priced item (含於第3項, 併…計價).
- **No quoted price.** Both cells are empty or zero (number or text 0), or hold only 不計價, 業主供料, 不含估價 or 另案.
- **Inside a panel.** The line is in an R33 scope; it is a numbered descendant of a panel line (1 控制盤 → 1.1); its own cells state that it is in or for a panel of this contract (盤內, 裝於控制盤); or it supplies a panel or enclosure itself (P1). A flat-numbered sibling after a panel line is not inside it.
- **Rows.** A line with a quoted price, or inside a panel, is a demand item. Never exclude a line inside a panel for lack of a price.
- **Kept, never excluded.** The following stay demand items even without a quoted price, each with a review reason and a limitation:
  - a line that R42 holds (hold it and cite R45);
  - an R45 doubt: other price content (另計, 詳單價分析, 免費, a lone dash, a negative number, an error or a missing cache); a line whose price may be carried by a priced parent line or by an analysis block of this source; a line under a heading that names a panel but forms no R33 group; a panel line whose own price text says 業主供料 or 另案, and the lines inside it; or every line of a contract that has no line prices.

  An R45 doubt is not an R24 C/D doubt; evaluators declare before generation whether it counts (R13).
- **Excluded.** Any other line with no quoted price that is not inside a panel (no enclosure and no estimate, CRQA-11) is not a 未填 row. Classify every one of its cells, including its item number and continuation rows, as `excluded_supply`; this overrides R29 for formula caches on those rows. The reason begins "no enclosure and no estimate: not in the quoted supply (R45)" and names the condition: price cells empty, zero, or the quoted text. Write no item for it, and cite none of its cells in any item.

Examples:
- 電力監視器 RS485 | 台 | 1, price cells empty, outside any panel → `excluded_supply`.
- 無熔絲開關 3P50A | 只 | 2, price cells empty, in the scope of 三、控制盤 → demand item with A 控制盤.
- 流量計 | 台 | 1 | 業主供料, outside any panel → `excluded_supply`, with the reason naming 業主供料.

**R46 Analysis blocks without a parent demand item (O6).** A block runs from its heading to the next block heading or the end of the sheet; page and column headers and footers inside it keep their own roles.
- **No parent.** The block's referenced item is absent when no demand item of this response shares its reference code or item name; a reference to another block's component counts as absent. Classify every other cell of such a block, including its heading, as `analysis_unlinked` with the reason "analysis block for an item not among this draft's demand items (R46)". Write no component items or links for it, and cite none of its cells in any item.
- **One match.** R21 and R25 apply, with a held link unless unit and basis prove it.
- **Two or more matches.** The link is held.

**R47 Acronym glosses (O8).** A bracketed acronym or abbreviation without digits, written directly after the full name that it abbreviates (無熔絲開關(NFB); 自動切換開關(ATS) 3P160A → C 自動切換開關(ATS), D 3P160A), stays in C with its brackets. It is no longer an undecided class. A letter-only bracketed token stating a material, light source or current type (電纜(PVC), 燈具(LED)) is an R31 attribute designation (D).

## Creator practice and owner decisions R47b–R58 (046f, declared before any 046f generation)

These rules implement owner decisions O10–O20 (`research/guideline046_005/DECISIONS_OWNER_O10_O12.md`, `DECISIONS_OWNER_O13_O16.md`, `DECISIONS_OWNER_O17_O18.md`, `DECISIONS_OWNER_O19_O20.md`) and the historical 未填 creator's majority practice (`STUDY_CREATOR_PRACTICE_20261104.md`, `STUDY_SUPPLY_MARKERS_20261104.md`). They govern every earlier sentence, rule (R1–R47), P1/P2, NAMING_RULES and example where they disagree. Earlier text stays in force for source reading, raw and provenance mechanics, census roles (R11, R29, R45, R46), holds (R42), document references (R5: never in C or D), cost-line codes (R23) and anything these rules do not address. In particular R1–R4, R7, R14–R17, R26, R30–R32, R34–R38, R43, R47 (reversed by O20) and NAMING_RULES no longer decide C/D; P1, R33 and the group mechanism no longer decide A/B; R6 and the field-table K/M/N wording are replaced by R48; the Q adoption hold is replaced by R54; R56's naming of A and B was revised in 046g under O30; R45's row tests are replaced by R49–R51. R48 and R52 (with R53) apply to `analysis_component` items as well as to rows.

**R47b Sources (O17).** A job has up to two source documents: the company material list (材料清單, primary when present) and the contract. Rows, descriptions, units, quantities, prices, brands, models and status come from the 材料清單 when the job has one, and from the contract otherwise. A response is bound to exactly one source inventory: the 材料清單 when the job has one, otherwise the contract. Only the bound source's packet is given to the generator; the other document is not supplied, and no field may cite or copy from it (046f has no reference channel; `harness/README.md`). A doubt the bound source leaves (for example a missing dimension) stays a null with a reason or a review reason. The brand table (R55) is used only when the job has no 材料清單 (O17).

**R48 Job inputs (O13).** The inventory carries three job-input context facts: `context:case_identity` (internal case id), `context:customer_name` (customer full name) and `context:project_name` (project short name). Every item, each 未填 row and each `analysis_component` item, carries L, M and N copied exactly from them, each citing only its own context ref (L `context:case_identity`, M `context:customer_name`, N `context:project_name`). K, O and P stay null. Never infer M or N from a letterhead (R6 still holds for inference). Route v4.3, like v4.2, rejects every departure on a job packet (`R48_JOB_INPUT_REQUIRED`, `R48_JOB_INPUT_REF`, `R48_JOB_INPUT_VALUE_MISMATCH`, `R48_FIELD_MUST_BE_NULL`).

**R49 Quoted range.** Rows come from the detailed bill sheet that carries the sender's prices; summary sheets never yield rows. The quoted range runs from the first item line under the column header down to the row whose unit-price or amount column holds 合計 or 總計 (a 合計 written in the description column is a block total, not the end). A total labelled for a block (小計, 甲站合計) does not end the range. Repeated site sections and goods/labour subsections inside the range are all in it. Everything after the end row (the signature line, notes, later sections, later analysis blocks) is out of range. When several total rows follow one another (合計, then tax, then 總計), the first one ends the range. When no total row exists, the range ends at the last item line before the signature line or the end of the sheet. When every bill line has no price and no status, rows come from the analysis sheet instead (R51).

**R50 Which lines are rows.** Inside the quoted range, in source order, each of these lines gives exactly one row:
- a priced line (its unit-price or amount cell holds a number above zero, or text stating a positive amount such as 1,200元);
- a not-estimated line. A line is not estimated when its unit-price or amount cell contains 不含估價; cite that cell. A 以下不含估價 heading is not inherited: each line under it is tested by its own cells;
- the sender's unnumbered fee lines before the end row (freight, drawing, design or management fee). Fee lines are rows only when priced or not estimated.

Not rows: a zero-state line (a cached 0 or empty amount with no unit price and no status) and any other line with no price and no status (`excluded_supply`, reason per R45); block totals, subtotals, the end row, tax and negotiation lines (R11); headings; item-like lines out of range (`excluded_supply` with the reason "outside the quoted range (R49)"). A remark saying a line is not included or not used does not remove it. Never split or merge lines, never add rows. Supply and separate-contract markers (業主供料, 甲供, 業主自備, 業主自理, 另案, 另案發包, supplied by others) do not decide inclusion and are never copied into a field.

Provisionally (O10): a panel line marked owner-supplied is omitted together with the lines inside it only when no line inside it has a price inside the quoted range. A line is inside the panel line when it is a numbered descendant of it (3 → 3.1, 3.2) or lies in its R33 heading scope. Every cell of an omitted line, including its item number, continuation rows and a not-estimated line inside it, takes the census role `excluded_supply`, with a reason that names the marker and says "owner-supplied panel with no priced line inside the quoted range (R50, provisional O10)". When a line inside has a price, O10 does not apply and every line, the panel line included, follows the row tests above (`SYNTHETIC_ALLOCATION_CASES.json` A11, A12).

*Note for the owner (O7, O10, O11).* Under O11 the creator's majority practice decides rules that are not solid, so R50 supersedes O7 (R45): a line inside a panel with no price and no status is no longer a row, although R45 kept every in-panel line. The O10 record says "the panel group stays and its lines are rows"; that can be read as keeping the unpriced owner-supplied panel line itself as a row, which R50 does not. Until the owner decides, evaluators declare both readings of A12 before scoring (`harness/EVALUATOR_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`).

**R51 Analysis components as rows.** When a bill line inside the quoted range has no price and no status, has its own analysis block, and that block lies inside the quoted range, or when every bill line has no price and no status (the blocks are then on the analysis sheet), that block's components are the rows instead of the parent line: block by block, in analysis order, written as `demand` items. The parent line is not a row (its cells take the census role `section` with the reason "parent of analysis components listed as rows (R51)"), and the block's own 合計/計 rows are not rows. When the line's block lies outside the quoted range, the line is `excluded_supply` (reason per R45) and its block's cells are `analysis_unlinked` (R46). Component rows follow R52–R57 like bill lines: E and F are the component's own unit and quantity, Q its unit price, and I = J = 不含估價 when the component is not estimated. When the parent line is priced or not estimated, its components are never rows; they stay `analysis_component` items (R21, R25) or `analysis_unlinked` cells (R46). An enclosure component row is an enclosure row (R56); R25's "never A or B" applies only to `analysis_component` items.

**R52 C and D (O15, O20).** Segments are separated by ，,、；; only (whitespace does not separate segments). Join a separate specification cell to the description with '，'; join continuation rows with no separator. First drop the generic leading prefix word named in the glossary and its delimiter. Then drop the leading category chain: the segments before the item's own noun that only name a product category, that is a segment ending in 類 or a category word the glossary lists (`category_words`). Chain segments are dropped only while a later noun segment remains, so the last segment is never dropped as chain. The chain is dropped on every line type. The noun segment is the first segment left. Then apply in this order: glossary, acronym, single segment, head noun, other; the first that applies decides C.
1. **Glossary.** The noun segment begins with a device the glossary lists (take the longest such entry): C = its abbreviation; D = the rest of the description after that device name. A single glossary segment gives D = the rest, or null.
2. **Acronym.** On an equipment line, the noun segment writes a Latin acronym fused to the noun or in brackets after it: C = the acronym alone; D = the rest of the description after the noun and its acronym, or null when nothing remains (自動切換開關(ATS) 3P160A → C ATS, D 3P160A; O20). An acronym is a digit-free Latin token abbreviating the noun; material, light-source, current and protocol tokens (PVC, LED, AC, DC, RS485, Modbus) stay in D and do not decide C.
3. **Single segment.** What remains after the prefix and the chain is one segment: C null and D = the text.
4. **Head noun.** On an equipment line: C = the noun segment cut before its first digit or bracket; D = the rest of that segment and the later segments. When the noun segment's first token contains a digit (AC220V 指示燈, RS485電力監視器), apply rule 5 as for a description starting with a digit: C null, D = the text.
5. **Other.** Every other line: C null; D = the description without the prefix and the chain.

**Equipment lines** are lines whose head noun names a discrete, individually specified device of one of these families: switches, breakers, contactors, relays, timers, meters, instruments, surge protectors and arresters, pumps, motors, fans, lamps, sockets, transformers, starters. The list is exhaustive: no other noun is equipment, except that every device the glossary lists is equipment. **Other lines** include cable and wire, conduit, pipe and fittings, size-first valves, hardware, busbar, sundries, PLC modules and software, services, labour and fees, descriptions starting with a digit, enclosures other than the R56 enclosure row, and every noun not in the families above.

Only the prefix, the chain, C's text (the text C was taken from: the glossary device name, the noun with its acronym, or the head noun), markers (R50), references (R5, R19, R20) and cost codes (R23) leave the description; the rest stays verbatim except R53. On the enclosure row, R56 also removes the text A took from the line and the dimensions B was built from. A delimiter or whitespace left at the start or end of D is dropped. Remarks, code columns and price text never enter C or D; they stay in raw text.

Invented examples:
- 產品，控制開關類，選擇開關，30mm → C = the glossary abbreviation for 選擇開關, D = 30mm.
- 電磁接觸器 AC220V (one segment; the glossary lists 電磁接觸器) → C = its abbreviation, D = AC220V.
- 產品，電線電纜類，600V XLPE電纜，3C，2mm² → C null, D = 600V XLPE電纜，3C 2mm² (only the comma between the two rating tokens becomes a space, R53).

*Evidence basis (N1).* C null on bulk and catalogue material follows the orchestrator's creator-practice follow-up of 2026-11-04 (evaluation role, non-reserved tuning cases; the figures are as reported to the package author, who did not re-check them): C filled on 2 of 121 cable rows and 1 of 29 conduit rows, and similarly rare on other bulk and catalogue material; C filled on 205 of 221 equipment rows; C blank on 116 of 117 service rows. Where these per-class counts differ from STUDY_CREATOR_PRACTICE CD-04 ("on priced material lines, C is filled"), R52 follows the per-class counts.

**R53 Rating tokens.** A token is a run between whitespace or delimiters (R52), with brackets removed. A rating-like token is a short token made of digits with a unit or rating letter (2P, 20AT, 100AF, 25KA, AC220V, 220V, 160A, 3C, 2mm², φ30mm, 2a2b, 1.5t, M6), or a standard rating code (Ics, AC, DC). Material grades, protection codes and protocol designations such as SUS304, IP65 and RS485 are not rating-like. Wherever a delimiter sits between two rating-like tokens, D writes a single U+0020 instead: the delimiter and its whitespace become one space, and the wrapping brackets around those tokens are dropped: 2P，20AT，(AC，Ics) → 2P 20AT AC Ics; φ30mm，2a2b → φ30mm 2a2b. A delimiter next to a token that is not rating-like (附端子蓋, 固定螺絲, SUS304) stays. Other punctuation in D is kept verbatim.

**R54 Q (O16).** On a priced line, Q is the line's unit price as a number, cited from its unit-price cell, never the amount. Q does not wait for adoption to be verified; the route accepts it under O16. Q is null on not-estimated lines.

**R55 I and J (O14, O16, O17, O18).** On a not-estimated line, I = J = 不含估價, citing the unit-price or amount cell that contains it (R50). On any priced line, a brand (廠牌) or model (型號) stated in the line's own cells, including the source's brand and model columns, is copied. Otherwise, only when the bound source is the contract, on a priced equipment line, apply the table by device type and rating band; a breaker whose rating no entry covers takes J = the table's breaker brand (O18), I null. With a 材料清單, I and J come only from its cells (the enclosure row's J follows R56). A value from the table cites the description cell and `context:brand_table` (R58); the table's breaker brand is the brand its breaker entries carry (O18 gives them one brand). A contract line that states only a brand or only a model takes the missing value from the table entry for its device type and rating band only when the entry's brand matches: for a brand-only line the entry's brand equals the stated brand; for a model-only line the entry lists the stated model. Any other device or rating the table does not cover leaves I and J null with a reason. Priced material, service and fee lines with no stated brand or model leave I and J null.

**R56 Enclosure row (O16, O30; A and B revised in 046g).** R56 decides which line is an enclosure row and fills A, B, C, D, I and J on it. A and B appear on enclosure rows only (O30): every other row, including the devices, labour and fees listed with a panel, leaves them null.
- **Enclosures.** An enclosure is a 盤, 箱 or 櫃 that houses devices: for example a control panel, panel box, control box, meter box, instrument box, isolation box, sub-distribution box, grounding test box or terminal box (端子箱); the list is not exhaustive, only the listed non-enclosures are excluded. These are not enclosures: junction and pull boxes (接線盒, 接線箱, 拉線箱), an existing panel and work on it (既設盤), enclosure parts (a door, a base plate) and services on an enclosure (assembly, wiring, lifting, testing, a base). A priced line that supplies an enclosure itself is an *enclosure row*, one row in source order. A job may have several, and each is its own enclosure row. A not-estimated enclosure line is an ordinary row, not an enclosure row: no group, A and B null with a reason, C and D per R52, I = J = 不含估價.
- **Terms.** Segments are separated as in R52 (，,、；; only; whitespace does not separate). An *enclosure word* is 盤, 箱, 櫃, PANEL or PNL. An enclosure word directly followed by 體, 內, 面, 門, 座, 板, 架 or 殼 is part of another word and names no box (箱體, 盤體, 盤內配線, 盤面, 盤門, 電表箱座); any other enclosure word ends a *box noun*, the segment's text up to and including it (PANEL and PNL only as whole words, with no Latin letter after them). Exception: 體 after 箱 or 盤 at the end of a noun with text before the enclosure word (控制箱體, 不銹鋼電表箱體) does not stop the noun: it names a box, and its box noun includes 體. The bare words 箱體 and 盤體 — a segment that is only that word, or that word after only the generic prefix or a category segment — name no box; they stay category words (R52). A segment *names a box* when it holds a box noun (控制盤; 雙層式電表箱 SUS304; 甲-1 PANEL; 控制盤組). A *tag* is a designation of one panel: a code containing a digit or a Latin letter (CP-1, P1, MCC-A), or a placeholder 甲, 乙, 丙… that the invented examples use for one; a floor (1F, B1F), site (甲站) or material (鋼板) is not a tag. A *bare panel tag* is a segment that is a tag alone or a tag with PANEL or PNL (己 PNL).
- **A, panel section.** A section heading's *panel name* is its text without its item label ([2], <3>, 三、, or the number in its own cell), without a leading category segment (a first segment that only names a panel type or product category and is followed by a segment that names a box, such as 低壓配電盤，), and without a trailing 設備, work word 工程, 製作 or 更新 (R33's list) or count of enclosures (2面), removed repeatedly (丁2盤設備 → 丁2盤; 控制盤更新工程 → 控制盤; 動力盤P-1 2面 → 動力盤P-1). The heading *names a panel* when its panel name holds exactly one box noun and ends with it, or with it followed only by a tag or one bracketed qualifier (甲-1 PANEL; 乙 PANEL (丙站); PANEL 甲-1). Work, site, cost and part headings name none: 甲站機電工程, 設備費(項目), 控制盤及配線工程 (控制盤及配線 does not end with its box noun), 盤體設備 (盤體 holds no box noun), 配電盤及控制盤 (two box nouns). An analysis-block title (R51) names a panel when its last segment, without a block label ending in a colon (工作項目：), passes the same test; that segment, with the same removals, is the block's panel name.

  A heading's scope is as in R33: up to the next heading of the same or a higher level, a subtotal or total row, or the end of the table; when headings nest, the innermost heading that names a panel governs. A block title's scope is its block's component rows. Component rows (R51) are in no section heading's scope; only their block's title counts. When a heading or block title names a panel, its panel name is A of one enclosure row in its scope: the first enclosure row there that is not a subsidiary box (a box noun containing 電表, 電錶, 端子, 測試, 隔離 or 儀表), or the first enclosure row when every one is. Only enclosure rows count. Every other enclosure row in the scope takes A from its own line. An A taken from a panel name cites the heading or title cell:
  - `[2] 甲-1 PANEL` → 甲-1 PANEL;
  - `[1] 低壓配電盤，乙 PANEL (丙站)` → 乙 PANEL (丙站);
  - `<3> 丁2盤設備` → 丁2盤;
  - `壹、控制盤更新工程` → 控制盤;
  - block title `工作項目：低壓配電盤，一般屋外型，戊 PNL` → 戊 PNL;
  - block title `工作項目：控制盤` → 控制盤.

  Exception: when the panel name has no tag and that row's own box noun has one, A comes from the line (a tag is never lost). The row's tag counts whether it stands in the box noun (甲-1 控制盤), directly after it (控制盤 CP-1, step 2 below) or in a later bare panel tag that step 5 moves before it (己 PNL).
- **A, otherwise.** A is the line's own enclosure noun phrase, cited from the description cell:
  1. Drop the generic prefix word (產品, R52) and every leading category segment: each segment before the box segment that R52 drops as chain, and the glossary's enclosure word (箱體 in the invented glossary), in any order (產品，盤箱類，箱體，控制箱 → 控制箱). A word ending in an enclosure word and joined by a hyphen to a following box noun is dropped with the hyphen (低壓配電盤-丁盤 → 丁盤); a hyphen before a tag with no box noun belongs to the tag (配電盤-1 stays).
  2. Take the first remaining segment that names a box and cut it after its box noun (after the enclosure word that ends it, or after 體 under the Terms exception: 甲-2 控制箱體 → 甲-2 控制箱體), keeping a tag written directly after it, with or without a space or hyphen (控制盤 CP-1 → 控制盤 CP-1; 配電盤-1 → 配電盤-1). When the line names two or more boxes, take the last of them instead (甲端子箱(含銅排)，乙測試箱(含配件) → 乙測試箱); a box introduced by 附 or 含 (附電表箱, 含端子箱) is an accessory, not a second box (控制盤，附電表箱 → 控制盤). A bare panel tag after the box segment is not a second box (step 5). When no remaining segment names a box, A is the line's bare panel tag if it gives one; otherwise A is null with a reason, and the carrier group's name is null with a limitation (產品，箱體，烤漆 under a work heading).
  3. Keep the qualifiers and the tag; never reduce the noun to a generic word: 雙層式電表箱 SUS304… → 雙層式電表箱; 甲-1 控制盤 → 甲-1 控制盤. (046f dropped the tag, CP-1 控制盤 → 控制盤; 046g keeps it.)
  4. A parenthesis directly after the box noun stays in A when its text ends in 型 or 式 (控制箱(屋外型), 分電盤(壁掛式)) or is a tag (控制箱(P-1)); every other parenthesis leaves A: a dimension parenthesis goes with the dimensions B was built from, and a material, rating, inclusion or site parenthesis stays in D (液位儀表箱(SUS304) → A 液位儀表箱, D (SUS304); 甲盤(W:700，H:1800，D:500) → 甲盤). An opening bracket that the cut leaves unpaired at the start is dropped ((庚型電表箱，30A以上) → 庚型電表箱).
  5. A bare panel tag that the line gives in a later segment goes directly before the noun, with nothing between (分電箱，鋼板，己 PNL → 己 PNL分電箱).
- **B.** B = `<w>W*<h>H*<d>D`: the width, height and depth numbers as written, in millimetres, with no unit and no thickness. Take them only from dimensions labelled W/H/D or 寬/高/深 in the line's own description (the description cell, joined with a separate specification cell or continuation rows as in R52): `W:800，H:2000，D:600` → 800W*2000H*600D; `(W)600(H)1500(D)400` → 600W*1500H*400D; `-W300×H300×D150mm` → 300W*300H*150D; `寬600×高800×深250mm` → 600W*800H*250D. Numbers with no unit, or with mm or 公釐, are millimetres. B gives width, height, depth in that order whatever the source order (H1800×W700×D500 → 700W*1800H*500D). Only W, H, D, 寬, 高, 深 count (長 or L labels none). With two complete sets B takes the first and D keeps the rest. A leading dash and a × joining the depth to a thickness leave with the dimensions (W700×H1800×D500×2.0t → D 2.0t). A thickness (2.0t) is not part of B. B is null, with a reason, when the description states no such dimensions, when they are only in a remark, code or heading cell, when one of the three is missing, or when they are stated in another unit (cm, m).
- **C and D.** C = the glossary's generic enclosure word, citing the description cell and `context:glossary`. D = the line's description after removing the prefix, every leading category segment and the hyphen-joined word that step 1 drops (also when A came from a heading), the text A took from the line (only when A came from the line: the noun up to the cut with a tag kept after it, a kept type or tag parenthesis and a moved bare panel tag) and the dimensions B was built from, with their labels, separators and unit and with brackets that enclose only them. A removed part leaves with the delimiter after it (at the end of the description, with the delimiter before it). R53 applies to the rest, a delimiter or whitespace left at an edge is dropped (R52), and D is null with a reason when nothing remains. When A came from a heading or block title, the line's own noun stays in D.
- **Other fields.** E and F verbatim; Q = unit price; I null. J = the bound source's brand cell when it states one; otherwise the company name from the company resource (`context:company_name`), cited with the description cell.
- **Carrier group.** The enclosure row is carried by a one-row group (relationship `physical_enclosure`) whose `name` equals A and whose `specification` equals B, because the interface requires a group for A/B. Its `name_refs` cite the cell A came from (the heading, title or description cell), or nothing when A is null, and its `specification_refs` the description cell, or nothing when B is null. No other row carries A or B, and no other groups are formed. Route v4.3, like v4.2, checks the mechanism on a job packet (`R56_GROUP_RELATIONSHIP`, `R56_GROUP_ONE_MEMBER_REQUIRED`) and that a J citing `context:company_name` equals it (`R56_J_COMPANY_NAME_MISMATCH`); it does not detect enclosures or check how A and B were named.

Invented examples: `SYNTHETIC_ALLOCATION_CASES.json` A18, A19, A21, A31–A33 and A38–A49.

## Scoped packets R59 (046g, declared before any 046g generation)

R59 adds one mechanism for large 材料清單 workbooks. It changes no field-value rule; on a scoped packet it replaces the last sentence of R51 for blocks of priced or not-estimated parents (item 6). Where it and an earlier sentence of this package disagree (for example guideline §1 on missing sources or §2 on reading every sheet), R59 governs on a scoped packet.

**R59 Scoped packets.** A packet whose inventory carries the context fact `context:scope` is a *scoped packet*. Before generation the source preparer decided which sheets the job needs, and the inventory holds the cells and views of those sheets only. On a scoped packet, do exactly this:
1. **Read the fact first.** `context:scope` is JSON text (`cps.scope-fact.v1`). For every sheet of the workbook it gives `index`, `name`, `role` (`bill`, `analysis`, `summary`, `resource_statistics` or `other`), `visibility`, `rows`, `columns`, `content_cells`, `in_scope`, `reason` and `linked_analysis_blocks` (for an out-of-scope analysis sheet, the number of its blocks linked to in-range bill lines; 0 on every other sheet). It also gives `bill_sheet_index`, `bill_header_row`, the R49 end row `end_row`, the rule versions (`R59`, `BILL1.v1`), the threshold count and the SHA-256 of the scope digest, of the full inventory and of the frozen scope decision. The inventory's `sheets` carry the same `in_scope` flags (route `R59_SCOPE_FACT_MISMATCH`).
2. **Bill sheet and range.** The sheet `bill_sheet_index` is the R49 bill sheet and `end_row` is the R49 end row. Do not re-decide either. The quoted range runs from the first item line below `bill_header_row` to `end_row`, and R49–R58 apply inside it as on any packet. If the bill sheet's own cells seem to contradict the fact, keep the declared range, do not add or drop rows for it, and record the contradiction in `limitations`, naming the cells.
3. **Rows after the end row.** R49 still applies inside the bill sheet. Every content cell on a row after `end_row` takes the census role `excluded_supply` with the reason "outside the quoted range (R49, R59)", whatever it holds: a negotiated price, a signature, later sections, priced lines. No item, group or link cites such a cell; route v4.3 rejects one that does (`R59_ROW_BELOW_END_ROW`). The end row itself is `financial_summary` (R11). On a scoped packet this item overrides R11 for the cells after the end row: a signature or note there is `excluded_supply`, not `footer` or `commercial_note` as on an unscoped packet (the package example). Route v4.3 checks that no item, group or link cites such a cell, not which role it takes.
4. **Census.** Every content cell of every in-scope sheet takes exactly one role, as on any packet (`UNCLASSIFIED_CONTENT_CELLS`).
5. **Scoped-out sheets.** A sheet whose `in_scope` is false has no cells, views or census in the packet. It is not a missing, unreadable or incomplete source. It is no reason to hold under guideline §1, to give the response or an item the status `held` or `partial`, or to write a limitation that calls the source incomplete. No field, proof, raw selection, component link or review reason may cite, quote or reconstruct its content, and do not guess what it holds. Its cells do not exist in the inventory (`UNKNOWN_CELL_REF`).
6. **Analysis sheets.** An analysis sheet is in scope only when every in-range bill line has no price and no status, and its `reason` says so. R51 then applies: each block's components are rows, its parent line takes `section`, and the block's totals are not rows (A15, A36). Every other analysis sheet is scoped out. A separate analysis sheet lies outside the quoted range (A26), so its blocks give no rows: an in-range line with no price and no status whose block is on a scoped-out sheet is `excluded_supply` (reason per R45), exactly as on an unscoped packet (A37).

   On a scoped packet a block whose parent line is priced or not estimated gives no `analysis_component` items and no links. Classify its cells `analysis_unlinked` with the reason "block of a priced or not-estimated parent; its components are not 未填 rows and are not written on a scoped packet (R51, R59)". A block with no parent demand item follows R46. When the fact records `linked_analysis_blocks` on an out-of-scope sheet, route v4.3 warns `R59_LINKED_ANALYSIS_OUT_OF_SCOPE`. Those blocks give no rows: a priced or not-estimated parent line follows R50 as usual, and a parent line with no price and no status is `excluded_supply`. The first sentence of this paragraph can bite only on blocks inside the bill sheet itself, because an analysis sheet is in scope only when no in-range bill line is priced or not estimated. So the same bill sheet gives `analysis_component` items (R21, R25) on an unscoped packet and `analysis_unlinked` cells on a scoped one, depending on the workbook's size. Those components are not 未填 rows and are not scored.
7. **Never evidence.** `context:scope` is not field evidence. Do not cite it in any proof, raw selection, anchor, group or component link; route v4.3 rejects such a citation (`R59_SCOPE_FACT_CITED`). A field proven only by context, other than L, M and N, is still `CONTEXT_ONLY_PROOF_UNREPRESENTABLE`.
8. **Unscoped packets.** Without `context:scope`, every sheet of the workbook is in scope, as in 046f.

*Design decision (046g, 2026-11-05).* The basis is a source-only structure study of five large exposed tuning 材料清單 workbooks, counts only (cases 1131112, 1131207, 1140304, 1140607 and 1140808). Each has exactly one R49 bill sheet of 452–999 content cells. Up to 7,063 further content cells sit in unit-price analysis and resource-statistics sheets that yield no rows, and the census requirement of every in-scope sheet made these workbooks impossible for one reader to finish.
- *Threshold.* The scope stage applies when the full inventory has more than 2,500 content cells that are literal or cached-formula cells with content. Below that, every sheet stays in scope and there is no `context:scope`, exactly as in 046f (case 1140304, 2,459 cells, stays unscoped). Single-sheet workbooks cannot be reduced by sheet scoping, so a reader may receive an unscoped packet of up to 2,500 cells, or a scoped bill sheet larger than that.
- *In scope.* The BILL1 bill sheet, and an analysis sheet only when R51 takes the rows from it: every in-range bill line is unpriced with no status. The first draft of R59 also kept an analysis sheet when some unpriced, no-status in-range line had a linked block on it (its condition (ii)). That condition is dropped (2026-11-05): under R51 a block on a separate analysis sheet lies outside the quoted range (A26), so it added census cells but never rows. The study's option (iii), keeping an analysis sheet with any linked block, is not adopted. Components under priced or not-estimated parents are not 未填 rows and are not scored, so on scoped packets they are not written. For example, the 8 linked blocks of case 1140607 (1 priced and 7 not-estimated parents) stay scoped out, with the route warning.
- *Out of scope.* Summary, resource-statistics and other sheets.
- *Procedure (source preparation, frozen before any generation; `harness/README.md`).*
  1. Build and pin the full inventory.
  2. `harness/scope_tool.py` computes BILL1 and the R51 conditions on it and writes the scope digest.
  3. A fresh-context scope reader sees only the digest and R47b and R49–R51, and returns each sheet's role and flag, the bill sheet and the end row.
  4. `harness/scope_agree.py` requires agreement on the bill sheet, the end row and every flag. It freezes `SCOPE_DECISION.json`, or holds the case for the owner with no tie-break run. The digest shows the reader the tool's line and link counts, so the reader's agreement on the analysis flags is not independent of the tool; its independent part is the bill sheet and the end row. Reader doubts are recorded and do not hold a case.
  5. `harness/make_scoped_inventory.py` projects the full inventory: it removes out-of-scope cells and views, renumbers the kept views, sets the flags and appends `context:scope`, keeping every other record byte-identical. It then verifies the projection record by record.

  Zero or several BILL1 sheets hold the case. A range with no item-like line, or an all-unpriced range without an analysis sheet, also holds the case.
- *BILL1.v1.* Whitespace, including U+3000, is removed from cell text. A sheet is a candidate when:
  - (a) exactly one row has header cells whose whole text, after whitespace removal, equals 項次, a name word (項目及說明, 名稱, 品名 or 材料名稱), 單位, 數量, 單價, and 複價 or 金額;
  - (b) below that row, a cell in the 單價 or 複價 (or 金額) column reads exactly 合計 or 總計; the first such row is the end row;
  - (c) a number above zero stands in those columns between the header row and the end row.

  BILL1 found exactly the bill sheet and the study's end row in 5 of 5 study cases. Its known failure modes give zero or several candidates or a reader disagreement, and so hold the case: split or merged headers, vocabulary variants, several bill-like sheets, and unlabelled totals.
- *Limits.* Route v4.3 enforces items 1, 3 and 7 and gives the warning of item 6. It cannot see scoped-out cells, so a missed R51 sheet can be caught only by the scope stage and an audit that re-derives it from the frozen full inventory. If R51 were ever read to put a linked block on a separate analysis sheet inside the quoted range (A26 reversed), the scope condition would have to change with it.

Invented examples: `SYNTHETIC_ALLOCATION_CASES.json` A35–A37.

## Commercial and quantity distinctions

Keep supplied-by-customer, self-transport, no-estimate, original quantity and responsibility separate. Search all financial/remark cells for these clauses. A missing price alone does not prove nonresponsibility; the no-enclosure AND no-estimate condition must be established completely. A real zero remains zero. Unknown, explicit empty string and error are not interchangeable facts.

Retain services and costs that are actual source demands. Taxes, whole-project negotiation, management allocation and totals have financial/context roles, not additional supplies merely because a unit or number is present. Do not borrow an 已填-stage deletion convention.

For composition or bundled wording, preserve the count's referent without changing F or creating extra purchases. For analysis, record literal per-unit basis and source quantities separately; no multiplication/division without independently supported scope and an admitted policy.
