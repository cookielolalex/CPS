# Creator practice on supply and separate-contract markers (study, 2026-11-04)

This was an evaluation-role study of six non-reserved samples. The findings are generic; case evidence is kept privately. It answers owner decisions O10/O11.

**Sources.** Contracts for three samples, and the creator's working copy (原材料清單) for the other three. Two samples share a template.

**Unit of analysis.** 70 item lines carrying a marker, either on the line itself or inherited from a marked heading.

**Result.**

| Question | Evidence | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| What decides whether a line becomes a 未填 row | 70/70 marked lines | **Quote scope**, ignoring the marker |
| Does a marker on its own mean "exclude"? | fits 65/70 | No: it gets every in-quote line wrong |
| How are rows inside the quote filled when unpriced? | 2/2 | Ordinary C, D, E and F; I = J = `不含估價`; Q blank |
| How are priced enclosures under an owner-handled heading filled? | 3/3 | A = enclosure type; B = W×H×D in mm; D = material or thickness; E, F; Q = unit price; C, I and J blank |
| Is the marker text copied into any field? | 0/5 included lines | Never |
| Do marked headings, summary lines and notes become rows? | 0 of 19 | Never |

Quote scope works as follows:

- A line in the body of the creator's quote is a row, whether it is priced or explicitly 不含估價.
- Lines beyond the quote total, under a not-estimated heading (以下不含估價), or in unpriced sections outside the quote are not rows.

**Q-另案.** INSUFFICIENT at line level: no item line in the samples carries 另案, which appears only in a general-conditions clause. The nearest analogue, 業主自行發包, was excluded 19/19. All 19 lines were unpriced and sat in the not-estimated tail.

**Owner's tentative rule (O10).** It agrees with every analogue in the samples, but no direct instance exists:

- groups with a priced line inside the quote were kept (2/2);
- unpriced groups were excluded (6/6);
- vendor-supplied panels with only a reference price outside the quote total were excluded (3/3).

**Recommendation.** Decide inclusion by quote scope rather than by marker. Adopt O10 provisionally, defining a "quoted price" as a price inside the quote or contract body. Never copy a marker into a field, and output an item once when it is listed twice.

**Caveats.**

- Only six samples, and only five marked lines were included.
- Two samples share a template.
- Three sources are the creator's working copies, which she may already have edited.
- The heading-inheritance and ditto-mark calls were judgement calls.
