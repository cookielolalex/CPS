- **Equipment versus material:** a description starting with a size or number is material. Digit-first single segments go to D only on 84/89 rows.
- **The noun in C:** when no glossary abbreviation applies, the head noun is the whole first segment on 62/87 rows. On 16/87 it is that segment's leading part, cut at the first digit or bracket.
- **The category chain:** dropped from D when C is filled (49/52). The later 材料清單 study found it dropped on 84% of rows even when C is blank.
- **Single-noun lines:** a device family goes to C (35/36). Anything else goes to D only (162/200).
- **Overall fit:** the rule family agrees with the creator on 91% of these 740 rows.
