# Draft supply-name instructions for a contract-reading AI

Status: proposed generic rules for review and future testing; NOT a deployment-approved guideline. This resource supplements a complete CPS contract-reading policy and does not authorize additional sources. Use only the source packet authorized for the current generation task.

## Aim

Produce a source-supported supply/service description for drafters. The name may occur before or after codes, ratings or models, in another column, or on a proven continuation line. Find it from the complete logical item. Do not translate, shorten to a familiar abbreviation, correct spelling or supply a manufacturer/model from general memory.

## Field roles

- C 材料名稱: the source's identifying product, accessory, bundle or service noun phrase, including functional distinctions needed to say what is supplied. Preserve multiple identifying nouns when the source is a combined item. Preserve a service suffix when the line is a service.
- D 規格: supported technical attributes, ratings, dimensions, material/finish and included accessories that are not the identifying name. Do not discard text simply because its exact field role is unresolved. Keep the full source in ancillary evidence and flag uncertainty; do not pass unresolved content as a fully supported specification.
- I 型號: an explicitly supported model/type identifier. A code's letters, digits or position are not sufficient. An explicit model column/label is strong evidence; otherwise the authorized source must establish the model relation.
- J 廠牌: a supported manufacturer/brand role. Do not infer it from a familiar model or confuse it with an ordering supplier or customer.
- Panel tags/group names, unit/quantity, contract unit rate, supplier and administrative details have their own roles in the full17-column policy. A tag is not a model; a numeric technical rating is not an item quantity; a contract total/negotiation amount is not an item rate.

## Reading procedure

1. Establish item scope before naming. Read relevant headers, index hierarchy, all cells in the logical item, authorized remarks and proven continuations. Do not join a new numbered item or unrelated analysis row merely because it is nearby. Preserve source cell/page locations and raw typed values.
2. Identify what the line supplies or performs. Search the entire logical item for the head noun and its functional/compound modifiers. A blank description cell does not by itself prove there is no item. A description containing only a code requires checking the specification and continuation cells. A noun in a document-reference note alone does not prove that the referenced device is supplied.
3. Separate the following roles using context: lexical name/acronym; model/type; rating/dimension; material grade; protocol/interface; panel/equipment tag; standard/test code; brand; accessory inclusion; service; commercial note; source reference; unresolved text. Do not use a rule such as “letters and digits means model” or “everything after the first comma is specification.”
4. Keep the most specific source identity. An accessory for a contactor remains the accessory; an installation fee remains the service; a panel foundation remains the foundation. A broad category followed by a specific product noun should not lose the latter. Retain both noun spans in C unless the declared presentation policy explicitly assigns the category elsewhere while retaining traceability.
5. Allocate separable ratings wherever they occur: before, after, adjacent to, or between name spans. Do not remove a functional phrase merely because it includes a number. For example, a position-count modifier can distinguish a switch type, whereas a voltage rating is a specification. Keep a composition count attached to the noun it modifies; moving a bare count to D can lose its referent even when the evidence still mentions it. Do not turn that count into an item purchase quantity. An angle plus an elbow noun contains both a rating and an identifying noun; preserve the elbow identity.
6. Preserve literal language and source spelling. If a source names an item using an acronym only, retain it. If it supplies a Chinese name, retain that name. If it supplies both, retain both in the declared name/specification/evidence representation without inventing equivalence or turning the acronym into a model. Generic source prefixes follow the package's explicit presentation policy; do not silently remove them on preference alone.
7. An uncertain model-like prefix does not prevent recognizing a clear noun later in the row. Populate the supported noun, leave I unasserted, and retain the token with a source reference and unresolved-role reason. If the source clearly says the token is a technical attribute but not a model, use the prospectively declared D policy. If the noun itself is missing or ambiguous, retain the candidate for review rather than inventing a name or silently dropping the row.
8. Keep a span/role record for each allocation: source hash, sheet/cell or page/item, exact raw text, destination, transformation and any uncertainty. Multiple source spans may form a name, but their order and mapping must be explicit. Raw proof must come from the immutable source, not a rewritten output value.
9. Perform a residual check: every identifying noun, model-like code, technical attribute, accessory and service suffix has a documented destination or unresolved status. Check that neither a compound name nor a specific appositional noun was lost. Verify item boundaries and output coverage independently of naming.
10. Apply display aliases only when a separately approved, versioned policy authorizes the exact equivalence and context. Source-language output is the default. Keep the original name and alias authority visible in ancillary records. No approved alias entries are supplied by this draft.

## Special safeguards

`不含估價`, responsibility notes, discounts and whole-project negotiation are not technical naming evidence. Preserve commercial text in its declared ancillary role. It does not alone remove an item or prove who supplies it. Do not turn a document reference such as “see specification/drawing” into a filled specification value from memory.

Do not automatically split a combined item or multiply a quantity based on a name's count. Do not borrow a material name, model, brand or dimension from historical 未填, later drawings or an evaluator. The package's source authority and target exclusion rules apply before any output release.

## Reader self-check

Before returning a row, verify: the named object matches the supplied object/service; names after models/ratings were examined; specification-column/continuation nouns were considered; model and brand assertions have actual role evidence; all source meaning remains accounted for; no unauthorized translation or alias was introduced; and every unresolved field is explicitly marked for review. A review flag is not semantic acceptance.
