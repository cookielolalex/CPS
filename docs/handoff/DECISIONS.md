## Supply-list interpretation

未填 supplies the drafter with a material/supply list in the agreed format. Whole-project negotiation totals are not item rates and do not belong in extraction questions merely to reconcile project prices.

The adopted responsibility condition requires **both absence of an enclosure and absence of an estimate**. Missing price alone is insufficient to delete a row or assign responsibility.
